About Us · Since 1989

Education is the preparation for life itself.

MUSALE CLASSES, established in 1989, is a premier institute helping students from class 9th to 12th prepare for boards and various competitive exams like IIT-JEE, NEET, and NDA.

With the sole objective of meeting the long felt need of a center of scientific and basic knowledge, which would meet the challenges of the present and the future technical and medical advancements of the fast changing world, Musale Classes laid its foundation under the able hands of Prof B.Y. Musale Sir.

B.Y. Musale Sir designed special courses with the vision to provide proper education, guidance, and training to students aiming for success in IIT, NEET, NDA/NA, and other competitive exams to pursue their careers and contribute to society and the nation. We strive to develop students' critical thinking skills and enhance their IQ.

At MUSALE CLASSES, education is more than just the accumulation of facts; it is the preparation for life itself. We believe in cultivating the right attitude, ethics, and knowledge to shape students' personalities and equip them to face the competitive world.

Our assets include a skilled faculty with a proven track record and the unwavering faith of our students.

Our systematic curriculum helps students explore and maximize their intelligence. We assess student performance through regular evaluations, progress tracking, and adaptation, making exam success more attainable.

MUSALE CLASSES offers not only academic excellence but also a conducive study environment. Our staff is dedicated to helping every student excel.

      FAQ's

      Questions parents often ask.

      What are the prerequisites for joining a program at Musale Classes?

      There are no specific prerequisites. We welcome all students aspiring to excel in JEE, NEET, NDA, and MHCET.

      How can I schedule a campus visit?

      You can schedule a campus visit by contacting us at +91- 9011 58 4747 or emailing admin@musaleclasses.co.in.

      What is the duration of your programs?

      The duration of our programs varies. Please refer to our program pages for specific details or contact us directly.

      Do you offer any scholarships or financial aid?

      Yes, we offer scholarships based on merit and financial need. Please contact our admissions office for more information.

      What is the success rate of your students in competitive exams?

      Our students have consistently achieved top ranks in JEE, NEET, NDA, and MHCET exams. Please speak to us for detailed information.

      Do you provide hostel facilities for outstation students?

      Yes, we offer comfortable and secure hostel facilities for outstation students as part of our residential programs.

      We maintain small batch sizes to ensure personalized attention and effective learning for all students.
